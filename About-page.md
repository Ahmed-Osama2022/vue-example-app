# About Page Sections

NOTE: All this data are fetched from the backend

1. **Hero** — Badge, headline with gradient highlight, description, primary CTA, and email CTA.
2. **Stats** — Four key numbers with labels (founded, users, countries, uptime).
3. **Mission** — Section label, title, paragraphs, and a founder quote card.
4. **Values** — Section label, title, and four value cards with icon, title, and text.
5. **Timeline** — Section label, title, and a vertical list of milestones (year, title, text).
6. **Team** — Section label, title, description, and a grid of team member cards (avatar, name, role).
7. **CTA** — Title, description, email button, and a "Back home" link.
8. **Footer** — Copyright / footer note.
